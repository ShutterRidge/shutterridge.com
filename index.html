About

Shutter Ridge

Shutter Ridge is the work of Paul Johnson, a photographer based in Colorado. I'm drawn to quiet places and strong lines: a ridge at first light, a fire escape in late shade, a tower against open sky. My photographs move between the natural and the built world, looking for the moments where the two feel like they belong together.

Every print is a signed, numbered limited edition on archival fine art paper, made to last a lifetime on your wall. I also take on a small number of commissions each year for people and businesses who want their spaces seen with the same care.
